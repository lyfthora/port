NTS

[Built in 2026]

React Electron TypeScript CodeMirror
Github

I built NTS because every notes app out there felt like too much or too little. It's a desktop app that keeps all your stuff 100% local — no sign-ups, no syncing drama, just your notes on your machine. It comes with a proper Markdown editor, a drawing canvas for when words aren't enough, wiki-links to connect ideas, import and export in Markdown.
